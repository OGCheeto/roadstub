Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The short version: Roadstub doesn't collect your data. What you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Roadstub saves your shifts (dates, times, odometer readings, earnings, tips, orders, notes), car expenses (date, category, amount, gallons, odometer, vendor, notes) and settings (such as mileage-rate overrides and your business-purpose label) in your browser's local storage on your own phone or computer. It is not sent to Roadstub or to anyone else.

To work offline, the app also keeps a cached copy of its own files (the page, icons and styles) on your device. That cache contains no personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Roadstub has no account, no sign-in, no server database, and no way to see what you've entered.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Roadstub and this website currently use no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or third-party cookies. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first and the change will be described here.

Backups and exports

Backups (JSON) and spreadsheet exports (CSV) are files the app creates on your device when you tap the button. You decide where they go (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email). Once you move or share a file, the privacy of that copy depends on where you put it.

Hosting and purchases

The app and this website are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process standard technical information such as your IP address to deliver pages and keep the service secure; see GitHub's privacy statement. If you buy Roadstub, the purchase is handled by Gumroad, which processes your payment and email under Gumroad's privacy policy. Roadstub never sees your card details.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or remove all of it by clearing the website data for the Roadstub site in your browser settings (on iPhone: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data). Deleting the home-screen icon alone may not erase it. Download a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Children

Roadstub is meant for adult drivers and isn't directed at children.
